About AquaSense

AquaSense is a water-quality monitoring and decision support system for rivers in Norzagaray, Bulacan. It brings together field readings, sensor health, and alert monitoring in one dashboard so researchers and local teams can identify risk conditions early and act quickly.

Objectives

  • Track turbidity, pH, temperature, and TDS trends.
  • Monitor sensor availability and connectivity.
  • Provide evidence for safe-water decisions and reports.

Focus areas

  • Surface water monitoring
  • Environmental risk assessment
  • Research-ready reporting
